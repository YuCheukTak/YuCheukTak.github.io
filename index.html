01 / Profile

An engineering
mindset built by making.

My DAT background combines design, science and hands-on construction. I am most interested in the point where a theoretical idea becomes a physical, measurable system.

I am a senior student specializing in the engineering track at Instituto Salesiano in Macau. Since Senior 1, the school's Design & Applied Technology curriculum has given me a practical foundation in prototyping, technical documentation and hardware-software integration.

My current direction is engineering study at National Taiwan Ocean University, building further on my experience with automation, sensor networks, mechanical design and embedded systems.

04 / Method

Design is
an iteration.

My projects are not built around the idea of getting everything right on the first attempt. The useful part is identifying what fails, understanding why, and changing the system.

01 / DEFINE

Understand the constraint

Break the task into physical, electrical, software and time limitations before building.

02 / DESIGN

Make a workable system

Translate requirements into a mechanical layout, circuit, control logic or testable prototype.

03 / TEST

Find the weak point

Use physical testing and debugging to expose conflicts that are difficult to see on paper.

04 / REFINE

Change the design

Reinforce, reposition, retune or rewrite until the system is more stable and practical.
